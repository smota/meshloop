# Contributing to Meshloop

Read AGENTS.md and the engineering workflow. Changes must identify the problem,
acceptance criteria, relevant ADRs, validation, and actual AI executor/reviewer.
AI authors implementation and tests; humans may supply requirements, review, and decisions.
Do not include credentials, private prompts, or third-party code of unknown provenance.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion
are provided under Apache-2.0 as described in section 5 of LICENSE. Contributors retain
their applicable rights; this policy is not a copyright assignment or a separate CLA.
Submit only material you are authorized to contribute and disclose third-party notices,
AI tooling used when known, and relevant provenance limitations. Tool usage is not
proof of copyright ownership. A future commercial dual-license model would require
separate rights analysis; it is not established here.

PRs should state scope, linked requirements/ADRs, tests and limitations, review identity,
and any compatibility/security consequences. Do not claim independent review for a
self-review. Do not merge or publish without the authorization applicable to the task.
